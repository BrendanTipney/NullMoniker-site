XR and Technical Design

From 2021 to 2024 I worked at Arcturus, a volumetric video company. I started out supporting the creators who used HoloSuite, then moved into technical design, where I designed and built XR games, hand tracked demos, and web AR experiences around volumetric captures of real people.

Quest 3 and Apple Vision Pro : Unity, C#, Shader Graph

Asset 15

Asset 15 started life as a volumetric video story. For the Quest 3 and Vision Pro version we kept the captures and the story, and almost nothing else. I redesigned it from the ground up as a full environment AR game that plays out in your actual room, controlled entirely with hand tracking and gestures.

Mixed RealityHand Tracking Game DesignShaders
  • Role. Main game designer and technical designer on the project.
  • Code. Wrote most of the gameplay code in Unity and C#, with one other developer.
  • Shaders. Built every custom shader in the game with Shader Graph.
  • Team. Turned designs into plans the team could build from, and helped run the project.

01 : Menu

Levels on the ceiling

Levels hang from nodes mounted on your ceiling. You reach up and pull one down to play it.

02 : Mode one

Logic gate puzzle

Move OR, AND, and NOR nodes around the room in 3D, connecting and breaking links until the circuit works.

03 : Mode two

Assembly puzzle

Pieces of a volumetric capture are spread around your space. Find them and put the person back together.

Looking Glass and Leap Motion : Unity, C#

Touchable Hologram

A trade show demo that put a volumetric capture inside a Looking Glass holographic display and handed control to whoever walked up. With a Leap Motion under the display, visitors could rotate, zoom, and pan the hologram with their bare hands.

Web AR : 8th Wall

AR Cards

Printed cards we handed out at trade shows. Point a phone at one and a volumetric character plays back standing on the card, and stays locked to it as you move the card around. Nothing to install: it runs in the phone's browser.

AR Shopping

Creator tools : HoloSuite and the Unity HoloSuite Player

Both Sides of the Tool

HoloSuite is Arcturus's software for editing volumetric video. For two years as Product Specialist I was the person creators called when something didn't work: over 100 customers, including enterprise accounts, editing captures and deploying them in Unity and Unreal. I wrote the docs and tutorials, ran onboarding and training, and worked the booth at AWE.

Later I helped develop HoloSuite and the Unity HoloSuite Player plugin, with a focus on fast, intuitive UI and controls. Hearing exactly where creators get stuck, then getting to fix it, is my favorite loop in this work.
